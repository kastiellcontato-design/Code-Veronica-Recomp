- Corrigidos blocos escuros e cortes incorretos na iluminação de paredes e do chão.

## English

### What's new in v1.1
- Fixed missing or distorted shadows cast by the lighter.
- Fixed incorrect dark blocks and lighting boundaries on walls and floors.

Update through the launcher or extract the complete port ZIP over your existing installation. The updater preserves saves, settings, mods, and caches. Start **Code Veronica.exe**.

Version 1.0 remains available in the release history. To roll back, close the game and extract the v1.0 ZIP over your installation, keeping your personal files.

The Veronica PKG Extractor and LiveSplit ZIPs remain at version 1.0 and do not need to be downloaded again. You must own your PS3 game copy. Original game files are not included; the port requires the extracted game folder rather than the PKG itself.
